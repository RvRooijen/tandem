A smaller reset that comes long before the last rite. When the season ends, the garden goes back to bare earth: the flowers, the beds and the blooms are gone. The hole, the gnomes' story and the diary stay as they are.

Each reset moves on to the next season: spring, summer, autumn, winter, and round again. Something is kept from each season and makes the next one go better.

The last rite stays the second, bigger layer: it ends the owner and hands on the red hats.

Open: what is kept from a season, when a season may end, and whether each season plays differently.
