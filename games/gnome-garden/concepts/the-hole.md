The gnomes dig. How deep the hole is, is the clock of the whole game: new seeds need a depth, upgrades can only go as high as the hole is deep, and the last rite is at the bottom.

What comes out of it is called pebbles at first. They are not pebbles.

Each metre takes longer than the one before, by a table that is tuned so the game takes the time it should.
