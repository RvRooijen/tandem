At first a gnome is only an ornament. It stands by the flowers and gives the garden a bonus, and that is all. Nothing walks.

Later they do things, but only when you cannot see: at night, behind shut eyes. By day they stand exactly as ornaments stand: on their spot, in their standing pose, not mid-stride and not bobbing up and down by a pixel. You never see one move during the fade into night or out of it; by morning they are back before you look.

Much later you catch one moving by day, once, and that should be a shock.
