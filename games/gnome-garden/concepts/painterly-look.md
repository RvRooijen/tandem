The art is small but painterly, after the style notes of testp: more pixels than a classic sprite, soft clusters of colour instead of hard outlines, a light that comes from somewhere. All of it is drawn in pixeldraw, by its workers.

The light is testp's too: coloured pools around lamps that fall off in bands, a rim on the edges that face a light, shade on the sides that do not, shadows thrown away from it. By day there is one big source, the sun, and the colour of the light follows the time of day.
