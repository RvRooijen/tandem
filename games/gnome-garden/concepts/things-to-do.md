It is an idle game, but someone who wants to join in always has something to do: pick flowers by hand, pull weeds, catch what flits by, greet whoever comes through the gate, and use the round buttons, each of which needs a rest after.

There is a to-do list of three jobs. A finished job is crossed off in the diary, where it pays.

Joining in should be clearly faster than leaving it alone, but never the only way.
