In the beginning you simply sleep at night, like the nap you can take yourself: the eyes go shut and you see nothing. In the morning there are flowers on the doorstep and you do not ask how.

Nights are long enough to matter. Most of the early progress happens while you sleep.

Later a touch in the night wakes you. Later still you stay awake and watch the night go by, and then you see what they do.
