One number, from a hundred down to nothing. It is never spent or bought; the story takes it away.

Everything about how the game feels hangs on it: the colours of the page, the words on the buttons, the title, how often it rains, the fog, what the night looks like. At a hundred it is a seed packet. Near nothing it is somewhere else.
